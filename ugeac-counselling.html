If you are planning to pursue B.Tech admission in Bihar, UGEAC 2026 (Under Graduate Engineering Admission Counselling) is the primary counselling process through which eligible candidates can secure admission to government and private engineering colleges in the state. The counselling is conducted by the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) based on candidates’ JEE Main 2026 scores and ranks.

What is UGEAC?

UGEAC (Under Graduate Engineering Admission Counselling) is a state-level engineering counselling process organized by BCECEB for admission to B.Tech and B.E. programs in Bihar. Candidates who have qualified JEE Main 2026 can participate in UGEAC to get admission in participating engineering colleges across the state. Seat allotment is based on JEE Main rank, category, candidate preferences, and seat availability.

UGEAC 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Before applying, make sure you meet the eligibility requirements prescribed by BCECEB.

Educational Qualification

Candidates must have passed Class 12 (Intermediate) or an equivalent examination with:
  • Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects
  • One of: Chemistry / Computer Science / Electronics / Information Technology / Biology / Informatics Practices / Biotechnology / Technical Vocational Subject / Agriculture / Engineering Graphics / Business Studies / Entrepreneurship

Minimum Qualifying Marks

Category Minimum Marks Required
General (UR) 45%
Reserved Categories 40%
Aggregate percentage is calculated based on the qualifying subjects.

Alternative Qualifications

  • Diploma holders: Passed a 3-year diploma with at least 45% marks (40% for reserved categories).
  • D.Voc candidates: Passed D.Voc stream in the same or allied sector.

UGEAC 2026 Registration Process

The application process is completely online.

Step What you do
1 Online registration on the official UGEAC portal using personal details.
2 Fill application form: personal info, academic details, contact details, category details.
3 Upload scanned documents: photograph, signature, and required certificates (as per format).
4 Pay counselling fee via debit card / credit card / net banking / UPI.
5 Verify and submit the application form.
6 Download and save the confirmation page for future reference.

UGEAC 2026 Counselling Process

The counselling process is conducted online and generally consists of two rounds.

  1. Registration for counselling: Register online and complete fee payment.
  2. Merit list preparation: Prepared using JEE Main rank, eligibility, and reservation rules.
  3. Choice filling & locking: Select preferred colleges and branches, then lock choices.
  4. Round 1 seat allotment: Based on rank, category, choices, and seat availability.
  5. Admission (Round 1): Download allotment letter, report as instructed, and complete verification.
  6. Round 2 counselling: For candidates without a seat in Round 1 or those seeking upgradation.
  7. Final admission: After verification and fee payment, admission is confirmed.
