About Us

Welcome to College Geek, your trusted source for educational updates, entrance exams, admissions, counselling guidance, college information, scholarships, and career-related resources.

Our mission is to simplify the admission and counselling process for students by providing accurate, easy-to-understand, and regularly updated educational information. We understand that navigating entrance exams, counselling procedures, eligibility criteria, important dates, and college admissions can be confusing. That's why we aim to present all essential information in one place.

Our Purpose

At College Geek, we are committed to helping students make informed academic and career decisions. We cover a wide range of educational topics, including:

  • Engineering Admissions
  • Medical Admissions
  • Entrance Examinations
  • Counselling Processes
  • Government and Private Colleges
  • Scholarships and Financial Aid
  • University Admissions
  • Career Guidance
  • Education News and Updates

Our goal is to ensure that students and parents can access reliable information without spending hours searching through multiple sources.

What We Do

We research, compile, and publish educational content based on publicly available information, official notifications, admission brochures, university announcements, and trusted educational sources.

Our content may include:

  • Exam Notifications
  • Eligibility Criteria
  • Application Processes
  • Important Dates
  • Counselling Procedures
  • Admission Guidelines
  • College Information
  • Scholarship Updates
  • Frequently Asked Questions

Our Commitment to Accuracy

We strive to keep all information accurate and up to date. However, educational policies, admission rules, and counselling schedules may change at any time.

For this reason, we always encourage visitors to verify important information through official websites and official notifications before making any admission-related decisions.

Independence and Transparency

College Geek is an independent educational information platform. We are not affiliated with any government authority, examination board, university, counselling body, or educational institution unless specifically stated.

Our editorial decisions are made independently, and our content is created with the objective of providing useful and unbiased information to students.

Why Students Trust Us

  • Transparent editorial practices
  • Comprehensive admission and counselling guides

We continuously work to improve our content and provide valuable educational resources that help students achieve their academic goals.
