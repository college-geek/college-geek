Editorial Policy

At College Geek, our mission is to provide accurate, trustworthy, and up-to-date educational information for students, parents, and educators.

Content Standards

We focus on publishing content related to:

  • Entrance Examinations
  • Admissions
  • Counselling Processes
  • Colleges and Universities
  • Scholarships
  • Government Education Schemes
  • Career Guidance

Information Sources

Our content is created using information obtained from:

  • Official Government Notifications
  • Official Examination Authorities
  • University Websites
  • Admission Brochures
  • Public Announcements
  • Trusted Educational Sources

Accuracy and Verification

Before publication, content is reviewed to ensure accuracy and relevance. However, educational policies, admission procedures, and examination schedules may change.

Readers should always verify important information through official sources.

Corrections Policy

If any factual error is identified, we aim to correct it promptly after verification.

Users may report errors through our Contact Us page.

Independence

Our editorial decisions are independent and are not influenced by advertisers, sponsors, educational institutions, or third parties.

Sponsored Content

If sponsored content or promotional material is published, it will be clearly identified as sponsored content.

Transparency

We are committed to maintaining transparency, accuracy, and integrity in all content published on our website.
